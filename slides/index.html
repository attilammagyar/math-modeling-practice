Students' Dropout and Academic Success

Task: a university wants to provide early support for students at risk of dropping out.

https://attilammagyar.github.io/math-modeling-practice
https://github.com/attilammagyar/math-modeling-practice

Data

  • 4424 students who should have graduated already.
  • Features:
    • application-time info,
    • first 2 semesters results.
  • Outcomes:
    • graduated: 50%,
    • dropped out: 32%,
    • still enrolled: 18%.
  • No inconsistencies, NaN's or other problems were found.

Research questions

What model to use?

  • Random guess (baseline),
  • k-Nearest neighbors,
  • Logistic regression (with elastic net),
  • SVM,
  • Decision tree, random forest,
  • Stacked (the above fed into a final predictor),
  • AdaBoost.

Problem formulation?

  • Dropout vs. non-dropout (32% : 68%).
  • Graduated vs. did-not-graduate on schedule (50% : 50%).
  • 3 classes (32% : 18% : 50%).

What metric to use?

  • Precision and recall are both important ⇒ F1.
  • Stratified CV?

Which features to use?

  • All.
  • Early features.

Experiments

  • Feature transformations:
    • numerical features were standardized,
    • categorical features → binary features.
  • Hyperparameter optimization. (11 neighbors, 7-10 level deep trees, AdaBoost with decision tree, Stacked model with logistic regression as the final classifier.)
  • 5-fold CV, with and without stratification.
  • Decision function tuning.

Best results (min, mean, max)

All features

  1. Stacked on "did not graduate in time":
    • F1: 0.836, 0.854, 0.866.
    • Precision: 0.84, 0.865, 0.896, Recall: 0.804, 0.844, 0.869.
  2. Logistic regression on "did not graduate in time":
    • F1: 0.819, 0.852, 0.88.
    • Precision: 0.862, 0.883, 0.909, Recall: 0.772, 0.825, 0.867.
  3. SVM on "did not graduate in time":
    • F1: 0.834, 0.848, 0.868.
    • Precision: 0.821, 0.84, 0.871, Recall: 0.828, 0.856, 0.896.

Early features

  1. SVM on "did not graduate in time":
    • F1: 0.74, 0.746, 0.754.
    • Precision: 0.667, 0.679, 0.693, Recall: 0.806, 0.827, 0.851.
  2. Stacked on "did not graduate in time":
    • F1: 0.732, 0.743, 0.756.
    • Precision: 0.651, 0.679, 0.709, Recall: 0.761, 0.822, 0.849.
  3. Random forest on "did not graduate in time":
    • F1: 0.718, 0.74, 0.754.
    • Precision: 0.632, 0.661, 0.684, Recall: 0.83, 0.841, 0.865.

Observations

  • Stratification had no noticeable effect.
  • First two semester data is essential.
    • Models also used tuition fee timeliness and course selection (among others).
    • Note: university students have already passed admission requirements.
  • Threshold tuning was prone to overfitting.
  • No noticeable pattern in misclassified data.

When to convert 3 classes into 2?

  • It depends on the problem:
    • Did not graduate in time: no noticeable effect. (As long as the classes are balanced.)
    • Dropout: performance improvements.
      The Stacked model with the dropout vs. non-dropout problem came up to 3rd place when run on all features:
      • F1: 0.831, 0.851, 0.863.
      • Precision: 0.836, 0.86, 0.889.
      • Recall: 0.827, 0.844, 0.859.
